NODE 0.10.0 turns the trail into a canvas. The pages you read become cards you can arrange, link and group, and a trail can be replayed step by step or shared as a file. Thank you for testing.

NODE updates itself on Windows, and on a Mac from 0.9.6 on. Copies of 0.9.5 or earlier on a Mac need this version downloaded by hand once.

## What's new

- **The trail canvas is the start page.** NODE opens on your last trail's canvas. ⌘T, the + button and closing your last tab all come back to it. ⌘1 is the canvas, and ⌘2 to ⌘9 are your page tabs. ⌘E goes from a page to its card on the canvas and back.
- **Pages become cards by themselves.** A page becomes a card after 20 seconds on screen, when you scroll it by hand, capture from it, ask AI about it, or still have it open when you switch trails. Quick visits stay in the sidebar's timeline with their time on page. Use **+** or drag a timeline row onto the canvas to add one yourself. Select a card and choose **Remove from canvas** to take one off. New cards go next to the page they came from, with a line between them.
- **Canvas tools.** The toolbar at the bottom of the canvas has Select (V), Hand (H), Group (G), Link (L), Question (Q) and Note (N):
  - Drag from one card to another to link them, then choose Supports, Contradicts, Answers or Raises.
  - Drag a box around cards to group them and name the group.
  - Click to add a question or a note.
  - Shift-click cards to select several, then choose **View in Mosaic**.
- **Search from the canvas.** Typing in the canvas search bar dims the cards that don't match. Press Enter to search the web: a search card goes on the canvas, and pages you open from the results go next to it. The same list offers **Ask about this trail** and **Private search**.
- **The sidebar is the only chrome.** The sidebar holds, from top to bottom:
  - the trail switcher;
  - Back, Forward and Reload;
  - the address bar;
  - your tabs;
  - the timeline;
  - the tools.
- **Pages fill the window.** Over a page, move the pointer to the left edge or press ⌘S, and the sidebar slides over the page. To change trail, swipe sideways with two fingers on the sidebar or use the switcher menu.
- **Private tabs.** ⌘⇧N opens a tab that is in no trail. Nothing from it goes into your history, trails or suggestions.
- **Zoom out step by step.** On a page, pinch with your fingers coming together to go from the page to Mosaic, then to the Gallery (every tab in the trail, to choose which to show in Mosaic), then to the canvas. Each pinch moves one level.
- **Share a trail as a file.** **Share** (top right of the canvas, or File → Share Trail…) saves the trail as a `.trail.md` file you can send with any app. You can add a note and choose a question to **Start here**. The file keeps your cards, groups and links, and reads as plain Markdown. Private address parts, tracking codes, sign-in pages and screenshots never go in.
- **Open a trail someone sent you.** ⌘O (File → Open Trail File…), or drop the file on NODE. It opens read only, with the sender's note and **Start here** in the sidebar. **Save as new trail** gives you your own copy to change. The sender's file is never changed.
- **Replay.** **Replay** (top right of the canvas) shows how a trail was built, one step at a time: each page, capture, link and group, with a line saying what happened. Use ← and → to step, Space to play or pause, and Esc to end. Files you open start on Replay.

## Your trails after updating

The first time 0.10.0 starts, it copies your trails into the new format. Your old trail files stay where they were. Older versions of NODE didn't record time on page, so NODE decides which old pages become cards from how you used them: pages you read twice or more, read for 20 seconds or more, took notes on, linked from a thread, or still had open. The rest stay in the timeline.

Things that moved:
- The new tab page is now the canvas.
- The beta survey is under the canvas's search bar.
- The agent, downloads and full screen buttons are in the Menu's header.
- The layout setting is gone.

## Which file to download

Scroll down to **Assets** and download one file:

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4 or later) | `NODE-0.10.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `NODE-0.10.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows | `NODE-0.10.0-win-x64.exe` |
| Linux | `NODE-0.10.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner and choose **About This Mac**. If the line "Chip" says Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, download the Apple chip file. If it says "Processor" with Intel, download the Intel file.

You can ignore the other files, including **Source code (zip)** and **Source code (tar.gz)**. They are for developers.

## Installing on a Mac

If you have 0.9.6 or later, NODE updates itself: when the update is ready it offers to restart. Otherwise:

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
2. Drag **NODE** into the **Applications** folder, replacing the old one if you have it.
3. Open NODE from Applications.

NODE is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app. Copies of 0.9.5 or earlier can't update themselves; download this version by hand once.

## Installing on Windows

1. If your browser warns about the download, choose **Keep**.
2. Open the `.exe` file. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer.

Windows copies of NODE check for updates and install them by themselves.

## Installing on Linux

Download the `.AppImage` file, make it executable (right-click → Properties → Allow executing, or `chmod +x`), and open it.

## Known limits

- Trails you change in 0.10.0 don't show those changes if you go back to 0.9.10. Your trails from before the update are still there.
- Share links (Create Link) still use the older trail format: they carry pages, threads and notes, not the canvas layout. To share the canvas, use **Save File**.
- Trail files from 0.9.x open in the preview as before, with **Open as New Trail** and **Merge**. Merging is not available for the new canvas files yet.
- Screenshots and voice notes are not included when you share a trail.
- The first time NODE saves a password on a Mac, macOS may ask whether NODE can use its "Safe Storage" key in your keychain. Click **Always Allow**.
- Signing in to Google accounts opens in a separate Chrome-based window.
- On Windows, NODE is not listed under "Open with" for Markdown files. Use File → Open Trail File…, or drag the file onto NODE.
- On a Mac, passkeys (Touch ID / iCloud Keychain sign-in) are turned off; sites offer password sign-in instead.

## Reporting a problem

In NODE's menu bar, choose **Help → Report a Problem…**. NODE copies its diagnostics and opens a short bug form on GitHub. Paste them where the form asks, with what you were doing when it went wrong.
