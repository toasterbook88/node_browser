Principle

Shell for behaviour

NODE treats research as a behaviour you share with the people you research with.

Knowledge networking

From your trail to theirs

Research moves between people as a trail, so they see what you read, how it connects and what is still open.

  1. 01Read

    Pages you spend time on become cards on your trail’s canvas.

  2. 02Map

    Group the cards and link them as Supports, Contradicts, Answers or Raises.

  3. 03Send

    Share the trail as one file, with a note and a question to start from.

  4. 04Open

    They open it in NODE, read only, with your note and the question beside the canvas.

  5. 05Continue

    They replay how you built it, then save it as their own trail and keep going.

Features

Find the next source

Every trail is a canvas.

Pages become cards by themselves, next to the page they came from.

The next search, already typed.

Suggestions come from the page you are reading, not from an ad auction.

Ask the trail. Local or cloud.

Ask AI about a page or a whole trail with a local model or Grok, and page text is sent only when you ask.

  • Questions

    Put open questions on the canvas and mark them resolved with a conclusion.

  • Mosaic

    Up to 4 tabs on screen at once, with media playing in all of them.

  • Private tabs

    A private tab goes in no trail and leaves no history.

  • Research agent

    Ask a question and get a short report with numbered sources.

  • Talk

    See the Hacker News and Bluesky posts that link to the page you are reading.

  • Site rules

    Set autoplay, sound, Mosaic and blocking for each site.

  • Passwords

    Save and fill logins, encrypted by your computer's own secure storage.

  • Agents

    Claude Code, Codex and Cursor can search and read through NODE, read-only.

Privacy

What NODE sends

Stays here
History, trails, passwords and the local model stay on your computer.
Sent when you ask

Blocked
Ads and trackers are blocked with uBlock Origin's default lists.
Never read
Sign-in, banking and payment pages are never read or sent.
Never shared
Sign-in pages, private parts of addresses, tracking codes and screenshots never go in a shared trail.

Mosaic

Four tabs, all playing

Tile your tabs in a grid and up to four play together, with sound from all of them if you want it.

  • Sound: all

    Hear every tile at once, or only the one you clicked.

  • Centre on click

    Click a video and it scrolls to the middle of its tile.

  • A fifth tab

    The tab that started playing first pauses and is marked ⏸ in the tab bar.

  • Picture in picture

    Pop a video, or a YouTube or Vimeo player, into a floating window.

Autoplay

It plays when it opens

The main video or song on a page starts as soon as the page loads, including YouTube, Vimeo and Dailymotion players.

Paused stays paused
Pause something and NODE leaves it paused.
Per site
Set autoplay on, muted or off for each site.
Ads
Ads and trackers are blocked with uBlock Origin's default lists.

Share

Send the whole playlist

Send your trail of videos and pages to a friend as one file, and they open it in NODE with your note on top.

  1. 01Watch

    Videos and pages you spend time on land on your trail.

  2. 02Send

    Share the trail as one file with a note.

  3. 03Replay

    Your friend steps through what you found, in the order you found it.

Screenshots and sign-in pages never go in a shared trail.

Questions

Before you switch

Do Chrome extensions work?
No. NODE is built on Electron, which can't run them. Ads and trackers are blocked with uBlock Origin's default lists, built in.
What does NODE send, and to whom?
Nothing goes to the maker of NODE: there is no account and no usage data. Searches go to the search sources you turn on, and page text goes to Grok only when you ask it something.
Does the other person need NODE?
To open the trail as a canvas and replay it, yes. The file itself is plain Markdown, so anyone can read it in a text editor.
Can I sign in to Google?
Yes. Google pages open in a separate window of a Chromium-based browser on your computer, because Google blocks sign-in inside Electron.
Why does Windows show a warning?
The Windows build is not signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The Mac app is signed by Apple.
What happens to my trails if NODE stops?
They stay on your computer as plain Markdown files, and the code is MIT licensed, so your work stays readable without NODE.
Is it free?
Yes. No account, no paid plan.
